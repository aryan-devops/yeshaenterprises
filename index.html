Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best HDPE pond liner for fish farming in India?

For most commercial fish and shrimp ponds, a 500-micron (or 300 to 500 GSM) UV-stabilized, virgin-grade HDPE pond liner is recommended. Yesha Enterprises supplies custom-cut certified liners ready for immediate delivery.

What size ring blower is needed for a Biofloc fish tank?

A standard 10,000-liter (4-meter diameter) Biofloc tank typically requires a 1 HP to 2 HP ring blower. For multiple tanks, a manifold distribution system connected to a 3 HP or 5 HP blower provides optimal aeration.
